We collect headlines and publisher excerpts from public RSS feeds every 15 minutes. Full articles always live with their original publishers.

How the shortlist works

We select up to ten practical stories from the past seven days. Stories useful to PMs, developers, and designers together come first, followed by PMs, developers, then designers. Mixed two-discipline stories join the earliest applicable group. Explicit keyword rules on publisher titles and excerpts estimate relevance; these labels can be imperfect. Within each group, relevance, recency, and publisher diversity determine order. General coverage without a practical discipline signal is excluded, even if that means fewer than ten stories. Audience filters include shared stories relevant to that discipline. Exact duplicate titles and links are removed.

One longer read

MIT Technology Review and AI Snake Oil provide the analysis pool. We recommend one article from the past 30 days, using the same audience priority, then relevance, recency, and excerpt length. This is a simple editorial heuristic, not an AI quality assessment. Excerpts come from publishers; we do not generate summaries.

A small community

Votes are limited to one per article per browser. Click your vote again to remove it. Votes and comments are shared across readers and stored on this server. An anonymous cookie remembers your votes; no account is required. This version has basic comment rate limits and no moderation dashboard.
